About

A short account of who keeps this casebook.

By trade I investigate what computers were made to hide — misconfigurations, stolen credentials, the quiet paths an intruder takes through a network. By temperament I prefer the same method Holmes did: observe first, theorise never, and let the evidence do the accusing.

This site is a running record of that work — write-ups of research, the reasoning behind it, and the odd dead end that taught me something anyway.
